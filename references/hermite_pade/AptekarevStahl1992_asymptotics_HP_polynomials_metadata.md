# Aptekarev & Stahl 1992 — Asymptotics of Hermite–Padé polynomials

## Bibliographic record

- **Authors:** A. I. Aptekarev, H. Stahl
- **Title:** *Asymptotics of Hermite–Padé Polynomials*
- **Venue:** Chapter 6 in *Progress in Approximation Theory*,
  A. A. Gonchar and E. B. Saff (eds.), Springer Series in
  Computational Mathematics vol. **19**, Springer-Verlag New York,
  pp. 127–167 (1992)
- **DOI:** 10.1007/978-1-4612-2966-7_6
- **ISBN of book:** 978-1-4612-7716-3 (eBook 978-1-4612-2966-7)

## Why this paper is load-bearing for v0.2

This is the standard reference for the "Stahl-type" convergence theory
of Hermite–Padé approximants: an account, with Aptekarev (Steklov)
contributing the Russian-school equilibrium-problem machinery and
Stahl contributing the general meromorphic-extremality framework, of
the *n-th-root weak* and (for Nikishin / Angelesco systems) *strong*
asymptotic behaviour of Hermite–Padé polynomials. The chapter sets
out the vector potential-theoretic equilibrium problem on a system of
contours and proves that the appropriately normalised Hermite–Padé
polynomial sequences distribute their zeros according to the
equilibrium measure, with the consequent uniform convergence of the
type II approximants on compact subsets of the complement of the
contour system to the meromorphic functions being approximated.

This is the convergence theorem v0.2's ADR for shared-Q needs to *cite*
when it claims "Hermite–Padé approximants with shared denominator
recover the system's pole structure": the citation goes here, with
mention of the prior Gonchar–Rakhmanov 1981 vector-potential
groundwork that this chapter built on.

## Gap-3 closure verdict (Aptekarev / Steklov school)

**Does not close v0.2's gap, but supplies the convergence-theorem
citation v0.2 requires.** Aptekarev–Stahl 1992 is a pure
approximation-theory result: given a Markov / Nikishin / Angelesco
system of functions, the Hermite–Padé approximants converge on the
right domain with the right pole-identifying behaviour. The result
sits one level below v0.2's application: v0.2 applies Hermite–Padé to
the *Taylor jets of an analytic ODE flow* and reads off the *system's
movable singularities* from `Q`'s zeros. The theoretical guarantee
needed for that step is exactly the Aptekarev–Stahl convergence
machinery, but the *composition* (apply Hermite–Padé to a vector
Taylor jet of a Painlevé / Garnier / Noumi–Yamada flow, then bridge
poles) is not in this paper, and we have found no Russian-school
paper that performs that composition.

Mitigation: the 2019 López Lagomasino survey (now in this cluster as
`LopezLagomasino2019_intro_multiple_orthogonal_hermite_pade_1910.08548.pdf`)
re-proves the type-II uniform-convergence statement for Nikishin
systems and cites the Aptekarev–Stahl chapter; that suffices as the
*pedagogical* citation for v0.2 ADR purposes. The 2013/2014 papers in
this cluster (Fidalgo–López Lagomasino–Medina 2013 `1310.7010`, López
Lagomasino–Medina 2014 `1406.3737`) cover the rational-modification
case (i.e. Nikishin systems perturbed by rational functions) and are
even closer to the v0.2 setting where the system has finitely many
poles.
